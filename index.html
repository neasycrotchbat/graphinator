GRAPHINATOR

Animated chart generator for After Effects

Where controls live: data, chart type & formatting are edited here — press Update to apply. After generating, colors, timing & scale are live on the >> GRAPH CONTROLLER << layer (select it → Effect Controls). Manual edits to generated layers are lost on Update.
